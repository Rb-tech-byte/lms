Enhancement of the Moodle Learning Management System for Short Courses and MOOCs at the Open University of TanzaniaIntroductionThe Institute of Educational and Management Technologies (IEMT) is a core unit of the Open University of Tanzania (OUT) responsible for a wide range of ICT services. These include the development and maintenance of information systems, management of ICT infrastructure, hardware and network support, e-learning services, instructional technology, digital innovation, ICT consultancy, and capacity building. In addition, IEMT delivers 15 short courses aimed at strengthening basic ICT skills and supporting professional development. Over the past 10 years, these programmes have been delivered in 14 regions of Tanzania, reaching nearly 10,000 learners through face-to-face training.The increasing demand for flexible learning, rapid technological advancement, and the need to expand access beyond geographical boundaries have created the need to modernize the current training provision model. Learners now expect online access to courses, flexible enrolment, secure online payments, interactive learning, and digital certification.To address these needs, IEMT proposes to enhance the existing Moodle Learning Management System (LMS) with a view to providing a modern digital platform for delivering short courses and Massive Open Online Courses (MOOCs). The proposed Moodle Enhancement Framework introduces key improvements in learner experience, digital content delivery, online payments, learner engagement, certification, analytics, security, and system integration, while acknowledging possible accessibility considerations including both personal inhibitors (health and sensory access needs) and internet and technology availability (geographical/environmental). The framework is supported by a phased implementation approach that enables the Institute to deploy the enhancements progressively, manage costs and risks effectively, and establish a scalable, sustainable, and high-quality digital learning ecosystem that expands access to lifelong learning opportunities across Tanzania and beyond.The Moodle Enhancement Framework therefore aims to transform the existing Moodle Learning Management System into a modern platform that supports:Professional short coursesMassive Open Online Courses (MOOCs)Online enrolment and paymentsInteractive and mobile learningDigital certificates and badgesCorporate trainingLearner engagementLearning analyticsIntegration with other OUT ICT systems 
ObjectivesThe framework aims to:Increase access to Basic and Professional ICT short courses.Allow learners to study anytime and from different locations.Improve the Moodle learner experience.Support online registration, enrolment and payments.Improve the quality and interactivity of online courses.Provide automatic digital certificates and badges.Improve monitoring of learner progress and course performance via Analytics.Support corporate and institutional training programmes.Improve learner communication and engagement.Establish a sustainable platform for lifelong professional learning.Target Learning ModelsThe enhanced Moodle platform should support two main types of learning:A. Professional Short CoursesThese will typically be structured professional courses that may include, but are not limited to:Course feesFacilitator supportAssessmentsPractical activitiesCompletion requirementsDigital certificates/badgesLimited group cohortsB. MOOCsMOOCs will provide opportunities for large numbers of learners to participate in online learning.They may include:Free enrolmentSelf-paced learningOpen access learning materialsOnline assessmentsOptional certificates/badgesLarge learner communities

ConclusionThe proposed Moodle Enhancement Framework provides a practical roadmap for transforming the delivery of IEMT short courses into a modern, flexible, and learner-centred digital learning ecosystem. By implementing the proposed enhancements, the Open University of Tanzania will improve access to quality training, enhance the learner experience, increase operational efficiency, and expand its reach to learners across Tanzania and beyond. The framework therefore positions IEMT to sustainably support lifelong learning and strengthen the University's contribution to national digital skills and professional capacity development.
